1. Getting Started

Logging in

1 Open your browser and go to https://odooerp.co.ke.
2 Enter the email address and password you were given. First time? Use the invitation email’s “Accept invitation” link to set your password.
3 Staff land on the main apps screen — click Property Management. Tenants and landlords land directly on their portal page.

Finding your way (staff)

MenuWhat you do there
Property DetailsAll buildings and units — addresses, photos, who lives where, unit status
Tenancy DetailsAll rental contracts — rent, deposits, schedules, invoices
Tenant DetailsThe tenant register — contacts, documents, their contracts
ReportingPrintable reports: contracts, expiries, income, rent status
ConfigurationSettings (managers only): property types, rent types, labels
Searching: every list has a search box — type a tenant name, unit number or phone number and press Enter. Use Filters and Group By to slice lists (e.g. Group By → Property to see contracts per building).

2. For Property Managers & Front-Desk Staff

2.1 Registering a New Tenant

1 Property Management → Tenant Details → New.
2 Fill in: full name, phone, email, occupation. Attach a copy of their ID/passport (drag the file into the attachments / chatter area).
3 Save. The system automatically creates their contact card and portal login — the tenant receives an email invitation to set a password.
Use the tenant’s real email address — rent reminders and invoices are emailed there.

2.2 Creating a Tenancy (Rental Contract)

1 Property Management → Tenancy Details → New.
2 Tenant: choose the tenant. Property: choose the exact unit (e.g. Kamere Towers — A-203), never the whole building.
3 Enter the Rent amount, choose the rent frequency (Monthly), set Start Date and End Date (e.g. one year).
4 Enter the Deposit amount agreed.
5 Attach the signed lease agreement (PDF/photo) in the chatter.
6 Click Create Rent Schedule — the system lists every rent due date for the whole contract.
7 Click Start. The contract is now active and the unit shows as occupied.
One contract = one unit = one tenant. If a family or company takes two units, make two contracts.

2.3 Collecting the Deposit

1 When the tenant pays the deposit, open the tenancy and click Deposit Received.
2 The deposit is recorded against the tenant — you can always see it with Open Received.
3 It stays held until move-out (see 2.7).

2.4 Monthly Rent: Invoicing & Receiving Payments

Sending the rent invoice

1 Open the tenancy → Rent Schedule tab. Each line is one month.
2 On the due line, click the gear ⚙ (Create Invoice) button. An invoice is created for that month’s rent (plus any penalty or recurring charges).
3 Click the arrow ➜ (Open Invoice), check it, then Confirm and Send & Print — the tenant gets it by email and sees it on their portal.
Tenants also get an automatic reminder email when rent is due — no action needed from you.

Recording a payment

1 Open the invoice (from the schedule line or Accounting) and click Register Payment.
2 Choose how they paid (bank, cash, M-Pesa via bank), the amount and date → Create Payment.
3 The invoice shows Paid and the schedule line is marked paid. The voucher can be printed for the tenant.

2.5 Late Payments & Penalties

  • If a rent line passes its due date unpaid, the agreed penalty is calculated automatically and added when you invoice.
  • To see all arrears: Tenancy Details → Rent Schedules, filter Unpaid, Group By Tenant.
  • The Rent Voucher report prints a statement of paid/unpaid months for one contract — useful for conversations with tenants.

2.6 Handling Maintenance Requests

1 Maintenance app → New (or the tenant reports it and you log it).
2 Choose the Property (unit) — the tenant fills in automatically. Describe the problem, pick the team (Plumbing, Electrical…) and a priority.
3 Drag the card across stages as work progresses. The tenant and the property manager get an automatic email when work is In Progress and when it is Done.
4 If the tenant must pay for the repair (damage, not wear-and-tear): on the finished request click Create Invoice and confirm it like a rent invoice.

2.7 When a Tenant Moves Out

1 Make sure all rent invoices are issued and settled (invoice any final amounts, e.g. damages).
2 On the tenancy click Close (or Cancel Tenancy if breaking early).
3 Click Deposit Return to pay back the deposit (after deducting damage invoices if any).
4 The unit automatically shows as Available again, ready for the next tenant.

2.8 Renewing a Contract

1 Open the expiring tenancy and click Renew.
2 Enter the new period (and the new rent if it changed).
3 A fresh rent schedule is generated — continue invoicing as before.
Run the Contract Expiry report monthly (Reporting menu) so renewals never take you by surprise.

2.9 Paying the Property Owner (Landlord)

1 Landlord Details: each owner has a landlord contract listing what they are owed (their share of collected rent).
2 On the due payout line click Create Landlord Invoice — this makes a bill in the owner’s name.
3 Accounting pays the bill like any supplier payment; the owner sees it on their portal.

3. For Tenants (Portal Guide)

Share this page with your tenants — it is written for them.

Your tenant account

1 You will receive an email invitation from your property manager — click the link and set a password.
2 Log in any time at https://odooerp.co.ke.

What you can do

  • See your rental contract — rent amount, start and end dates, your deposit.
  • See and download your invoices — every month’s rent invoice appears under Your Invoices; download the PDF for your records.
  • Check what you owe — unpaid invoices are clearly marked. Pay by your usual method (bank transfer / M-Pesa as instructed on the invoice) and your manager will receipt it.
  • Get reminders — you’ll receive an email when rent is due, and a receipt confirmation when payment is recorded.
  • Report a problem — email or call your property manager to log a maintenance request; you will receive an email when the work starts and when it is finished.
  • Update your details — keep your phone and email up to date from your portal profile (top-right menu → My Account).
If you cannot log in, use “Reset Password” on the login page, or contact the property office.

4. For Landlords / Property Owners (Portal Guide)

Share this page with property owners whose buildings you manage.

  • Log in at https://odooerp.co.ke with the invitation sent by the management company.
  • Your properties — you see the buildings/units you own: occupancy, current tenants, condition records.
  • Your payouts — every rent share due to you appears as a bill in your name; you can see what has been paid and what is upcoming, with downloadable PDFs.
  • Statements — request a periodic statement from the management office (income collected, fees, maintenance deducted, net paid to you).
  • Privacy — you only see your own properties and documents; other owners’ data is never visible to you.

5. Frequently Asked Questions

The tenant paid two months at once — what do I do?
Create the invoices for both schedule lines, then register one payment across both invoices (Register Payment from the first invoice, then allocate the remainder to the second, or register two payments).
I invoiced the wrong amount.
If the invoice is unconfirmed, edit it. If confirmed, use Credit Note on the invoice and re-issue correctly.
A tenant disputes the penalty.
Penalties come from the contract’s late-fee terms. A manager can edit the penalty amount on the schedule line before invoicing, or credit it afterwards.
Can two people share one unit?
Put the lead tenant on the contract and add the co-occupant’s details in the contract notes/chatter. (True co-tenant billing is on the enhancement roadmap.)
How do I move a tenant to a different unit?
Close the current tenancy (deposit can be returned or carried), then create a new tenancy on the new unit.
Who can see what?
Staff see everything per their role. Tenants see only their own contract and invoices. Owners see only their own properties and payouts. Agents see their deals and commissions.
Something shows an error on screen.
Take a screenshot including the red error text and send it to your administrator (peterkamere@gmail.com) with a note of what you clicked.
