3. Test scenarios

T1. Rent invoicing and payment

  1. Property Management → Tenant Details → Tenancies; open Grimfield - Alice Wanjiru.
  2. Open the Rent Schedule tab. Six months are paid, one is invoiced but unpaid (overdue), last month and the current month are not invoiced yet.
  3. On the current month line click Create Invoice, then Confirm the invoice and Pay it.
Expected: The line shows Paid; Total Rent stays 14,400.

T2. Late-payment penalty

  1. Alice's tenancy has Penalty 5 % after 5 days (Penalty tab).
  2. On last month's line (not invoiced, more than 5 days late) click Create Invoice.
Expected: The invoice amount is rent + 5 % (1,260.00 for a 1,200.00 line) and the line shows the Penalty Amount.

T3. Overdue rent reminders

  1. Tenant Details → Send Rent Reminders (or select rent lines in a list and use Actions).
  2. Leave the list empty and click Send Reminders.
Expected: A notification reports the reminders queued; Settings → Technical → Emails shows a mail to alice.tenant@example.com. Tenants without email are reported as skipped.

T4. Deposit received and returned

  1. On Glohork Villa - Carol Mwangi (draft) click Start, then Deposit Receive and Confirm the payment.
  2. Click Deposit Return, confirm and pay the vendor bill.
Expected: Deposit Received? then Deposit Returned? become ticked; the property moves to On Lease.

T5. Contract expiry and renewal

  1. Reporting → Contract Expiry, range today to +1 month, Print.
  2. Open Dawson Drive - Brian Otieno (expires in ~3 weeks; all rent lines are invoiced), click Close, then Renew.
Expected: Brian's tenancy is listed in the report; the renew wizard proposes the new period.

T6. CRM rent lead to tenancy

  1. CRM: open 2-bedroom flat at Royal Manor (Frank Mutiso).
  2. Click Create Contract, set an end date, confirm.
Expected: A tenancy for Royal Manor is created; Frank becomes a tenant (contact + tenant record); the lead description is unchanged.

T7. Weekly rent

  1. Open Cleveland - David Kimani (Rent Type 1 Weekly, 300 per week).
Expected: The schedule has one line per week; the last part-week is charged at 300 / 7 per day. Recurring maintenance (500) is shown and added to rent invoices.

T8. Maintenance

  1. Property Maintenance → Maintenance Requests.
  2. Leaking kitchen tap is Repaired and invoiced to the tenant (renter's fault). Move Broken window in lobby to Repaired, set a cost and click Create Invoice.
Expected: The tenant/manager receives the done e-mail once; on the property form, Maintenance Cost and Net Income (after maintenance) update.

T9. Agent commission

  1. Commissions → Commission Invoice: COM/00001 (Kevin Mutua, 10 % of Brian's rent = 1,140.00) is invoiced.
  2. Reporting → Commission details report / Commission invoices report, wide date range, Print.
Expected: Both PDFs list Brian's tenancy and the 1,140.00 commission.

T10. Landlord payouts

  1. Landlord Details → Landlord Tenancy: Royal Manor - landlord lease (Lucy Achieng, 5,000/month).
  2. Three monthly owner bills are posted; on the next line click Create Invoice. Try Pay Deposit twice.
Expected: A vendor bill to Lucy is created; the second deposit click opens the same payment (no duplicate).

T11. Booking units

  1. Property Booking → Sub Property: Sunrise Towers has 12 units (3 floors × 4).
  2. Unit 1 is booked; click Make Available on it, then Book on another unit. Select two units of the same floor and use Merge.
Expected: Only the clicked unit changes state; merged units get a combined BHK label; units appear in the Properties list.

T12. Purchase and sale instalments

  1. Open Trek Rise: 24 monthly purchase instalments (1,800,000). Open White Villa: sale instalments from the payment term (2,500,000).
  2. Create a vendor bill / customer invoice from an instalment line.
Expected: The bill/invoice is created for the line amount; the company currency is unchanged.

T13. Reports and KPIs

  1. Reporting: Income & Expenditure, Tenancy by Property/Tenant, Document and Safety Certificate expiry (Fire insurance policy in 15 days, Gas safety certificate in 10 days), Property per Location, GFA / Occupancy / Operational cost / Investment analysis, Financial Performance Analysis.
  2. Open Grimfield and Dawson Drive: Occupancy Rate is between 0 and 100 %.
Expected: Every report opens or prints without error and shows the demo records.
